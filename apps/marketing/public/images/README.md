# Image Guidelines for KaziCloud Marketing

## 📸 Image Requirements

### General Guidelines
- **Format**: JPG for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency
- **Quality**: High resolution, professionally shot or sourced
- **Style**: Documentary, authentic, not stock-photo-looking
- **Color**: Natural tones, avoid oversaturation
- **Composition**: Clean, uncluttered backgrounds

---

## 📁 Directory Structure

### `/users/` - Professional Avatars (Features Section)
**Files needed:**
- `user-1.jpg` (400x400px)
- `user-2.jpg` (400x400px)
- `user-3.jpg` (400x400px)

**Guidelines:**
- Diverse professionals (different ages, genders, ethnicities)
- Professional but approachable headshots
- Neutral or office backgrounds
- Good lighting, sharp focus
- Square crop, centered faces

---

### `/process/` - Workflow Images (Process Section)
**Files needed:**
- `profile.jpg` (800x600px) - Person creating profile on laptop
- `matching.jpg` (800x600px) - Close-up of job search interface
- `working.jpg` (800x600px) - Professional collaboration/handshake

**Guidelines:**
- Candid, documentary style
- Show actual work scenarios
- Natural lighting preferred
- Avoid posed/staged looks
- Grayscale filter applied on hover (built-in)

---

### `/testimonials/` - Customer Headshots (Proof Section)
**Files needed:**
- `sarah.jpg` (200x200px) - Sarah Chen
- `marcus.jpg` (200x200px) - Marcus Williams
- `priya.jpg` (200x200px) - Priya Patel

**Guidelines:**
- Professional headshots
- Consistent lighting/style across all three
- Square crop
- Neutral backgrounds
- Genuine expressions

---

## 🎨 Image Sourcing Recommendations

### Free Stock Photo Sites (Use Sparingly)
- **Unsplash** - High quality, natural photos
- **Pexels** - Good for workplace scenarios
- **Generated Photos** - AI-generated faces (for privacy)

### Paid Options (Better Quality)
- **Stocksy** - Authentic, non-stock-looking photos
- **Death to Stock** - Unique, artistic workplace photos

### Best Option
- **Custom Photography** - Hire a photographer for authentic brand photos
- **Team Photos** - Use real team members (with permission)
- **User-Generated** - Ask customers for permission to use their photos

---

## ⚠️ What to Avoid

❌ Obvious stock photos (people pointing at laptops)
❌ Overly posed or fake-looking scenarios
❌ Low resolution or pixelated images
❌ Inconsistent lighting/style across sections
❌ Watermarked images
❌ Images without proper licensing

---

## ✅ Image Optimization

Before adding images:
1. **Resize** to exact dimensions needed
2. **Compress** using tools like TinyPNG or Squoosh
3. **Convert** to WebP for better performance (optional)
4. **Test** on different screen sizes

Target file sizes:
- Avatars: < 50KB each
- Process images: < 150KB each
- Testimonial headshots: < 30KB each

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Add your images to the appropriate folders
2. Name them exactly as specified above
3. Refresh the marketing site
4. Images will appear with grayscale effect (hover to see color)

**Note**: If images fail to load, fallback placeholders will display automatically.
